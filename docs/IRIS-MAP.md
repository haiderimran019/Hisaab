# IRIS field map

_Map version `IRIS-TY2026-TY2027.draft`. Generated from `src/iris.ts` by `tools/make-iris-doc.mjs` — edit the source, then run `npm run iris`. Do not edit this file._

**0 of 15 fields transcribed.** 12 required fields still unverified, so the map is a draft: `it-export-base`, `service-export-base`, `export-tax`, `export-withheld`, `domestic-turnover`, `business-expenses`, `business-profit`, `business-tax`, `domestic-withheld`, `wealth-statement`, `pseb-registration`, `return-filed`.

## Why the codes are blank

Hisaab names the part of the return and the section of law for every figure, but the exact IRIS field codes have not been transcribed from a live return yet. Use this to find your way around the return, not as a filing instruction, and check every box against what IRIS shows you.

A wrong field code does not show a wrong screen. It files a figure in the wrong box, which is a misdeclaration carrying the customer’s name — so no code here is ever written from memory, from a blog post, or from another tool’s output. The sections of law are different: those come from `docs/TAX-RULES.md`, which was checked against the Ordinance, and they are safe to rely on.

## How to transcribe one

1. Sign in to IRIS and **start, without submitting**, a return of income for the right tax year and taxpayer type (individual, non-salaried business).
2. Navigate to the path in the table below.
3. Copy the field’s code and its label **exactly as IRIS prints them**, including any odd capitalisation or spacing.
4. In `src/iris.ts`, set `code`, `irisLabel`, `verified:true`, and put your initials and the date in `transcribedBy`.
5. Run `npm run iris` and commit the source and the regenerated worksheet together.

Leave anything you could not find unverified. A blank is honest; a guess is not. Two fields are marked optional because a return may not show them at all: the s.4AB surcharge appears only above the threshold, and the s.154B row only from TY2027.

**IT / ITeS export tax base** — `it-export-base`, from `itBase`.

Where to look: Return of Income → the fixed/final tax schedule, the row for export of IT and IT-enabled services under s.154A

The gross amount realised through an authorized dealer, in PKR, before the dealer deducted tax. Hisaab holds this per receipt as taxBase and will not infer it from a net credit.

**Other export services tax base** — `service-export-base`, from `serviceBase`.

Where to look: Return of Income → the fixed/final tax schedule, the row for export of services other than IT / ITeS under s.154A

Separate row from IT/ITeS because the rate differs. Whether a service is IT/ITeS is a classification question, not something Hisaab decides.

**Tax on export receipts** — `export-tax`, from `exportTax`.

Where to look: Return of Income → the fixed/final tax schedule, the computed tax column against the s.154A rows

IRIS normally computes this from the base and does not accept it typed. Transcribe the code so the pack can be checked against what IRIS worked out.

**Social media and digital platform receipts** (optional) — `social-media-base`, from `specialBase`.

Where to look: Return of Income → the fixed/final tax schedule, the s.154B row for payments through social media and digital platforms

Hisaab does not treat this as automatically final and refuses to compute a liability for it. The entry exists so the pack can name the row and say a practitioner must decide.

| Tax already collected by your bank | **no** | — | — | 2026, 2027 | s.154A; s.168 |

**Tax already collected by your bank** — `export-withheld`, from `paidExport`.

Where to look: Return of Income → the tax-already-paid or adjustable-tax annexure, the row for tax collected on export proceeds under s.154A

Taken from the bank credit advice or the PRC, not from the invoice. A mismatch between this and the schedule above is the single most common reason a return is queried.

**Other tax already deducted** — `domestic-withheld`, from `paidLocal`.

Where to look: Return of Income → the adjustable-tax annexure, the rows for tax deducted by your Pakistani clients

Which s.153 row applies depends on how the client classified the payment. Hisaab records the amount and leaves the row to the filer.

**Domestic receipts** — `domestic-turnover`, from `localBase`.

Where to look: Return of Income → business income statement → gross revenue / turnover

Pakistani-client revenue. Export receipts are not included here; they sit under the final regime above.

**Deductible expenses** — `business-expenses`, from `expenseDeduction`.

Where to look: Return of Income → business income statement → total expenses / deductions

Hisaab applies the business-use percentage per expense. Apportionment between export and domestic activity is a judgement it flags rather than makes.

**Business profit** — `business-profit`, from `profit`.

Where to look: Return of Income → business income statement → net profit, carried to the computation

Turnover less fees less deductible expenses, floored at zero. Hisaab does not carry a loss forward and says so in its own note.

**Tax on business profit** — `business-tax`, from `business.base`.

Where to look: Return of Income → computation of tax → normal tax on taxable income

IRIS computes this. Transcribe the code so the pack can be reconciled against it.

**Surcharge above PKR 10 million** (optional) — `business-surcharge`, from `business.surcharge`.

Where to look: Return of Income → computation of tax → the surcharge line, shown only once taxable income exceeds the threshold

Only appears above the threshold, so it may be absent from a return you open to transcribe from. Leave unverified rather than guessing; it is marked optional for that reason.

**Return of income filed** — `return-filed`, from `declaration`.

Where to look: IRIS → Declaration → Income Tax Return, for the tax year being filed

Filing the return is itself one of the conditions for s.154A final-tax treatment, which is why Hisaab asks the filer to confirm it rather than assuming it.


### Wealth statement

| Hisaab figure | Transcribed | IRIS code | IRIS label | Years | Law |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wealth statement | **no** | — | — | 2026, 2027 | s.116 — wealth statement; s.116 reconciliation of assets |

**Wealth statement** — `wealth-statement`, from `declaration`.

Where to look: IRIS → Declaration → the wealth statement filed with the return of income

Hisaab produces no part of this. It holds income records, not assets, and the reconciliation needs everything a person owns. Named here so the pack can say plainly that the return is not complete without it.


### Registration / profile

| Hisaab figure | Transcribed | IRIS code | IRIS label | Years | Law |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PSEB IT / ITeS registration | **no** | — | — | 2026, 2027 | TY2027 rate card p.9 — the 0.25% rate applies where registered with PSEB |
| Active Taxpayer List status | **no** | — | — | 2026, 2027 | Tenth Schedule — higher rates for persons not appearing on the ATL |

**PSEB IT / ITeS registration** — `pseb-registration`, from `declaration`.

Where to look: Not in IRIS — PSEB maintains its own register at pseb.org.pk; the certificate is evidence kept with your records

This is the single largest swing in the product: 0.25% against 1% on the same base. Hisaab records it as a declaration and verifies nothing. Do not add a code here; there is no IRIS field.

**Active Taxpayer List status** (optional) — `filer-status`, from `declaration`.

Where to look: Not a return field — ATL status follows from having filed; check the published Active Taxpayer List

Affects withholding rates your clients and bank apply, not a box you fill in. Here so the pack can explain why a deducted amount differed from the headline rate.
