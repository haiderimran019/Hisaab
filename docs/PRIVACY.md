# Privacy and data use
_Updated 1 November 2026._
## Your books and your account
Guided bookkeeping works locally without an account. A random key encrypts income, expenses, invoices, statement lines, attached evidence, chats, memory, proposals and file snapshots. Your passphrase protects that key and is never sent to Supabase, Google or an AI provider. The unsaved demo lives in memory.
If you connect an account, Supabase holds your sign-in identity, company names and membership roles. If you choose Sync, it also holds an encrypted workspace envelope, its revision and update time. Owner and Editor roles can save within their company; Viewers can read its encrypted copy. The encrypted company workspace includes its chats and memory, so members who have its passphrase can read those too. Keep personal notes outside a shared company workspace. Membership alone does not decrypt books. Each new device still needs the workspace passphrase. Device passkey slots are removed from the cloud envelope.
Company access is enforced by database row-level security and membership tables. User-editable profile metadata cannot grant a company role. Conflicting revisions are refused. Loading a cloud copy replaces local books only after your confirmation and passphrase; keep an encrypted backup of unsynced work first. After a successful connection and sync, changes sync while the workspace stays connected and unlocked. Locking pauses cloud work and clears decrypted state.
## AI and your choices
Guided analysis and financial calculations use deterministic application functions. Optional on-device Qwen runs in a worker after a deliberate large download from Hugging Face and GitHub. Those hosts see your IP address, never your chats. The local development gateway can receive the current request and up to six shortened messages on localhost; it is not a public AI host.
The optional hosted assistant is reached through Supabase. Before each request, Hisaab shows the exact redacted current message and up to six recent chat messages, the model and its gateway recipient. Known client names, account references, emails and long identity/account numbers are redacted automatically. Redaction can miss details: check the preview. Only your Send tap transmits it. The company ID and sign-in token authenticate the gateway; they are not sent to the model. A ledger snapshot, attachment, passphrase or confirmed memory is not included.
The configured hosted route uses OpenRouter and its serving model provider, requests no data collection and zero data retention, and refuses paid model IDs. If an eligible free provider or quota is unavailable, guided tools remain available. Hisaab's gateway code does not store prompts or replies on the server; hosting and provider services process request metadata. Review the named provider's terms before sending confidential information. Private chats are not used for automatic shared-model training.
Hosted chat explains finance, economics, accounting and personal finance. It cannot save records, perform transactions, file returns or supply authoritative tax numbers. Record changes require your confirmed proposal. Tax calculations and IRIS answers use the checked rule register. User-given codes stay labelled From you, not verified and can be removed in What I remember.
## Reminders and notifications
If you enable background reminders, Supabase stores a browser push endpoint, delivery keys, your user/company IDs, selected tax year and reminder preferences. A private scheduled sender uses your browser vendor's push service. These capability URLs and keys are protected by row-level security and are not exposed to other users or companies.
Lock-screen reminders contain generic check-ins, never client names, balances or tax amounts. Your operating system controls their font and popup layout. You can turn them off in Account settings; signing out removes this account's subscription on this device. Selecting Never touch the network first attempts to revoke this device's reminder subscription, then blocks future app cloud requests. Other devices keep their own notification preferences. Calendar-file reminders are an alternative and are controlled by your calendar app.
## Other network requests
- Google sign-in, when configured and chosen, shares identity/profile information with Google and Supabase. It requests no Gmail or bank access.
- Supabase account, company, sync and assistant availability requests start when you choose Connect in Settings. Authentication tokens and non-financial sync-binding metadata are kept in this tab's session storage; decrypted records and memory are not.
- Reference exchange rates are fetched from open.er-api.com only when requested. The provider sees your IP address, not your ledger.
- Fonts, icons and app code are served by the website. Offline app caches contain public assets, never financial records. First-time model/export downloads need a connection.
The optional legacy Worker can separately handle consented usage pings, email subscriptions or document transcription when explicitly configured. It rejects customer ledger fields. A document-reading screen must name its recipient and request your consent before sending a chosen document.
Never touch the network blocks optional application requests and model downloads. Loading or updating the website itself and browser-managed push infrastructure are not network-isolation mechanisms. Already-loaded on-device inference can continue locally.
## Retention, deletion and limits
Local books remain until you delete browser data or the workspace. Exported files remain wherever you save or share them and contain plaintext financial data. Deleting a local workspace does not delete an existing cloud copy. Company/account deletion and member-management workflows require owner administration; the app does not yet offer self-service deletion of cloud companies. Ask the owner to remove a cloud copy and revoke access, and retain your own encrypted backup first.
Supabase retains the current encrypted workspace copy, account and membership records while configured. Reminder preferences last until revoked; expired push endpoints are removed. Quota records hold counts and a rate-window time, not conversation text. Google, Supabase, browser vendors and the selected AI provider each process their part under their own policies.
Hisaab is not connected to FBR or your bank, cannot submit a return or move money, and does not guarantee tax eligibility or complete current-law coverage. Review estimates with a qualified practitioner. Encryption cannot protect an unlocked or compromised device, and Hisaab cannot reset a forgotten workspace passphrase.
